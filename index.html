Welcome, Scientists & Mathematicians! 🔬📐

This is your museum. Every exhibit is a working lab — click anything, drag anything, poke anything. Wander the Waves wing to ride sound and light, or step into Shape Explorer to forge polygons with your hands. Over time, Mrs. Guyer (and whichever curious humans she pulls in to help) will fill these halls with new wonders across science and math.

Why a Hall of Discovery?

Because the best way to understand something is to play with it. These exhibits are full of levers, sliders, knobs, and clickable mysteries — the kind of science museum you wish you could live inside. Built by the REDS community, for the REDS community. No wifi required. No limit on wonder. 🌟
