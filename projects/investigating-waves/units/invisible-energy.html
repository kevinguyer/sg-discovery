Unit 04 · The waves you CAN'T see

Invisible
Energy

Right now — no, seriously, right now — your body is being flooded with waves. Wifi. Radio stations. Sunlight. Microwaves from the kitchen. Even X-rays from distant stars. You can't see any of them. But they're doing everything. ⚡

Concept 01 · The electromagnetic spectrum

Visible light is a tiny sliver.

All those "invisible" waves — radio, microwave, infrared, UV, X-ray, gamma — are the exact same thing as the light your eyes see. They're all electromagnetic waves. The only difference? Wavelength. Radio waves can be miles long. Gamma rays are smaller than an atom. 🤯 Scroll across to meet them all. 👇

Lab 01 · Radio tuner

Tune the dial. Catch the signal.

Radio stations broadcast by wiggling invisible radio waves at a specific frequency. Your radio's dial picks out just one frequency and turns it back into sound. Try tuning to find the stations! When you lock on, the signal gets strong and clear. 📻

Lab · Vintage Radiodrag the tuning knob
Try this: sweep the dial slowly. When you're between stations, you get static (random noise). When you hit a station exactly, the wave becomes clean and you can "receive" the program. That's what a radio does — millions of times a second!
Lab 02 · Microwave oven

How microwaves pop your popcorn.

A microwave oven is basically a tiny room that shoots radio waves with a very specific wavelength — one that makes water molecules shake like crazy. All that shaking = heat. Your food cooks from the inside out. No fire, no flame, just wiggling water.

Lab · Popcorn Microwavepress START · watch the H₂O dance
Watch: the little H₂O molecules inside the kernel wiggle faster as energy pours in. When water hits 100°C it turns to steam. Inside a tough kernel shell? 💥 POP! That's literally how popcorn works.
Lab 03 · X-Ray scanner

See through skin with high-energy waves.

X-rays are waves so short and powerful that they can zip right through soft stuff like skin and muscle — but they get stopped by dense stuff like bones. So doctors shine X-rays through you, and the shadow shows your skeleton. Slide to crank up the X-rays and take a look. 🦴

Lab · Medical X-Ray Imagerwarning: NOT a real medical device!
Important: real X-rays use a TINY dose — just enough to take the picture. Too many is bad for your cells! That's why you only get X-rays when a doctor needs them, and they use lead aprons to block the rest. 🩻
Did you know?

Six wild invisible-energy facts.

📡

Wifi is just radio.

Wifi, cell phones, Bluetooth, your car key, baby monitors — all use radio waves. Your devices just wiggle them really fast to send data.

🌞

You're glowing right now.

Your warm body gives off infrared light — you're invisible to regular eyes, but to an infrared camera you glow like a lightbulb.

🗼

Radio waves can be MILES long.

The wavelength of an AM radio station can be longer than the Empire State Building. Giant waves, tiny information — they wrap right around the Earth.

🐝

Bees see ultraviolet flowers.

Flowers have secret UV patterns we can't see — landing strips pointing to the nectar! Bees see them just fine. Science thinks it's pretty!

🌌

The Big Bang still echoes.

About 1% of the "static" on an old TV tuned between channels was actually microwave radiation from the Big Bang — 13.8 billion years old!

🕶️

Superman vision is (almost) real.

Airport scanners, satellites, hospital MRIs — humans have built tons of machines that "see" in X-ray, infrared, radio, and more. Our eyes are just one tiny camera.
