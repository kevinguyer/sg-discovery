Unit 02 · Prisms, rainbows, & lasers

Light Waves

Light is a wave too — but it's so fast (186,000 miles every second) and so tiny (shorter than a virus!) that your eyes only see one thing: color. Let's slow it down, split it apart, and bounce it off mirrors. 🌈

SCROLL · LET THERE BE LIGHT
Concept 01 · The visible spectrum

Color is just wavelength.

Every color you see is a light wave with a different wavelength — the distance from one crest to the next. Long waves look red. Short waves look violet. Your eyes are just wavelength detectors. Drag the slider and watch. 👇

Live · Visible Light Spectrumevery color your eyes can see
Weird fact: light with a wavelength longer than 740nm is infrared — heat you can feel but can't see. Shorter than 380nm is ultraviolet — what gives you sunburn. More on those in Unit 04! ⚡
Lab 01 · The rainbow maker

White light is every color stacked up.

Sunlight looks white — but shine it through a triangle of glass (a prism) and POW: a rainbow pops out the other side. Each color bends by a different amount, so they fan out. Rotate the prism and aim the rainbow where you want it. 🔺

Lab · Rotatable Prismdrag the slider · watch the rainbow sweep
Try this: rotate the prism so the rainbow spreads widest. The colors split because violet light bends a tiny bit more than red light when it passes through glass — each color's wave has a different size!
Lab 02 · Color mixer

Light mixes backward from paint.

Mix red and green paint and you get… brown. But mix red and green light and you get yellow! Screens like your phone and TV use just 3 colors of light — Red, Green, and Blue — to make every color. Try it.

Lab · Additive Color Mixer (RGB)light adds up
Try this: set all three to 255 — that's white light. Set them all to 0 — that's total darkness. Mix only Red + Blue → magenta. Green + Blue → cyan. Your screen is doing this right now, millions of times per second!
Lab 03 · Laser maze

Angle in = angle out. Every time.

When light hits a mirror, it bounces off at the same angle it came in. That's the one rule — and it's how every telescope, periscope, and disco ball works. Drag the mirrors to steer the laser into the target. 🎯

Lab · Reflection Puzzledrag mirrors · click endpoints to rotate
Try this: grab the yellow dot at either end of a mirror to rotate it, or grab the middle to slide it. Get the laser to hit the target — the target turns green and confetti pops! 🎉
Did you know?

Six wild light facts.

🚀

Light is the speed limit of the universe.

Nothing can go faster than light — 186,282 miles per second. Light from the Sun takes 8 minutes to reach us.

🌈

Rainbows are always circles.

You only see half because the ground gets in the way! From an airplane, a rainbow is a full, perfect ring.

🦐

Mantis shrimp see SIXTEEN colors.

You see 3 (red, green, blue). Mantis shrimp have 16 color receptors. They see ultraviolet and polarized light!

🌌

When you look at a star, you're time-traveling.

Stars are so far away that their light takes years to reach you. You're seeing what the star looked like long ago.

☀️

Why the sky is blue.

Sunlight bumps into air molecules, and they scatter blue light more than other colors. You see blue from every direction!

🪲

Fireflies make light with no heat.

Most lights (like bulbs) waste energy as heat. Fireflies are 100% efficient — scientists are still trying to copy their trick to build better lightbulbs!
