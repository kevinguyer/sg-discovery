Concept 01 · The method

Cut it into shapes you already know.

A composite shape is a shape built out of simpler ones — an L, a T, a house, an arrow. Nobody has a formula for "house." But you have formulas for rectangles and triangles, and every composite shape can be sliced into those. Find the area of each piece, add them up, and the case is closed.

The detective can only measure three things:

▸ Rectangles (width × height)
▸ Squares (side × side)
▸ Triangles (base × height ÷ 2)

Anything else shows a ? — keep cutting until every piece has a number.

Lab 02 · Two strategies

Add the pieces… or subtract the bite?

Some shapes look like a rectangle with a bite taken out. You can cut them into pieces and add — or draw the whole box and subtract the missing bite. Both are right. One is usually faster. Switch strategies and compare.

STRATEGY DESK
pick a shape · switch between ADD and SUBTRACT

Count the pieces each strategy needs. Which one would you rather do on a test?

The picture frame is the clincher: adding needs four pieces, subtracting needs one big box minus one hole. Detectives choose the shorter route.

Bonus

Six wild real-world area facts.

🏠

Floor plans are composite shapes

Carpet sellers, painters, and tile layers measure rooms exactly this way: split the floor into rectangles, add them up, subtract the closet.

🗺️

Countries are polygons

Map software finds the area of a state or country by slicing its outline into thousands of thin triangles. Same trick, bigger scissors.

🍕

Pizza math

Which is more pizza: one 12-inch or two 8-inch? Slice them into tiny triangles and add — the single 12-inch wins (113 vs 100 square inches).

🎨

Paint by the square foot

A can of paint says how many square feet it covers. Painters measure wall area, subtract the windows and doors, and buy exactly enough.

🌾

Ancient tax collectors

In ancient Egypt and Babylon, land tax depended on field area. Scribes split odd-shaped fields into rectangles and triangles 4,000 years ago.

🎮

Video games count hits

To decide whether an arrow hit a dragon, games check if a point is inside a shape made of triangles. Area formulas run millions of times a second.
