For teachers

Virginia Explorer follows the 2023 Virginia Studies Standards of Learning (VS.1–VS.13) in chronological order. It is a reference and exploration tool, not an assessment: nothing is graded or saved. The only quizzes are geography self-checks (regions, rivers, bordering states, and, in Chapter 2, the Indigenous language groups). The map is generated from U.S. Geological Survey and U.S. Census Bureau data. Images are public domain and credited on each card.
